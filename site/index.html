Same rank.She has the better seat.Who gets to go?

Shalini and her twin Sonu share a JEE Main rank of 8,000. The women-only pool opens NIT Delhi to her; his best is NIT Durgapur. In our model, 23 in 100 village families like hers send her to Delhi, while 96 send him to his best college. model v2

Money hardly moves that number. The worry of how long it takes to reach her does. We built the model, a game where you play the parents, a video and a full report, and we show every number's source.
