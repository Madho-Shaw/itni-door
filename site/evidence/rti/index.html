Two RTI requests

Nobody publishes how many women and men who get a seat at a far college actually take it. We asked the two bodies that would know. Neither could give us the table. That gap is itself a finding, and we say only what the replies say.

IIT Kanpur: refused

Filed
21 October 2026
We asked
JoSAA choice-list totals, by gender and by home state or other state
Reply
5 November 2026, request refused

We got no table from this request, so we do not claim anything about what the data would show.

University of Delhi: answered in part

Filed
23 October 2026 (OA No. 5149/2026)
We asked
Undergraduate admissions by gender and home state, before and after CUET, and hostel seats
Reply
18 November 2026

What the reply says:

  • Admissions by home state are "not maintained".
  • The other counts we asked for are not held in that form, and the University declined to compile them.
  • One figure was given: in 2025, University hostels had 2,232 seats for women and 1,192 for men. Women hold about 65% of those seats.
  • College hostels are not counted centrally, so this covers University hostels only.

What this does and does not show

The hostel count tells us about beds, not about who chose to come from far. On its own it does not say whether women are held back or welcomed. What the two replies do show is that the admission side does not count, or will not share, who takes a far seat by gender and home state. So the question has to be answered from outside, from Census migration and counselling data, as in our report.

Copies of both requests and both replies, with the applicant's address, phone and e-mail covered, are pinned in our research diary.
