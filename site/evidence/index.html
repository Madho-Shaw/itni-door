How to read the marks

Sourced comes from a file or page we name. Calibrated was tuned to match one real number. Assumed is our choice, with a range. Dramatised is a line or scene we wrote.

What we keep off this site: admission lists with names, the original RTI scans (copies with the personal address covered are in our research diary) and social-media usernames. Licensed data stays local; the files here are our own summaries.
